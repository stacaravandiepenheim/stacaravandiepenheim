The mobile home

Our cosy mobile home is located at campsite De Mölnhöfte in Diepenheim, in the beautiful region of Twente, and is suitable for up to 4 persons. It is especially comfortable for 2 adults with up to 2 children, but also very suitable for guests who enjoy peace, nature, walking and cycling.

The mobile home has a living area with seating and dining table, a kitchen, two bedrooms, a bathroom with toilet and shower, and a covered outdoor seating area. There is also a fenced garden, a private parking space and a covered storage area for bicycles.

The mobile home is especially suitable for guests who appreciate comfort, a quiet setting and a pleasant base for exploring the area.

The campsite

Campsite De Mölnhöfte is located just outside the charming village of Diepenheim. It is a small and friendly campsite with facilities for both young and old. There are playgrounds, sports facilities and a restaurant with terrace. In season, extra facilities such as the swimming pool are available.

Adults can enjoy the peace and the countryside, while children can play on the campsite. Outside the holiday periods the campsite is often especially quiet.

The surroundings

Diepenheim is located between Zutphen and Enschede in the region called Hof van Twente. The area is known for its peace and quiet, nature, woods, heathland, beautiful cycling routes and the six castles around Diepenheim.

Near the campsite you will find, among other things, the historic Den Haller watermill, Warmelo Castle with its gardens and sand sculptures, several playgrounds, a restaurant, and possibilities for golfing, walking and cycling. Day trips to Delden, Markelo, Bad Bentheim or amusement parks are also easy to make.
